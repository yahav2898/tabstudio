TabStudio 0.10 runs your app on every screen at once: phones, tablets, and virtual devices of any size.

## What's new in 0.10

- **Swarm.**
  - Phones and tablets running TabStudio join your tablet's swarm with a QR code: **File > Join a Swarm** (or Swarm on the start screen) on the phone, and the **+** in **View > Swarm** on the tablet. Pairing is end-to-end encrypted with pinned certificates, over your local network.
  - Choose **Swarm** in the Run menu and press F5. The tablet builds once and every device shows the app full screen, with a small overlay naming the tablet. Avalonia apps run natively, web apps come from the tablet over the encrypted link, and console apps stream their output back with input from the tablet.
  - Every save updates every device. A device that fails never stops the others.
  - A breakpoint on any device stops in the tablet's debugger with that device's Locals and Call Stack. **Pause all on a breakpoint** stops every device together.
  - **Mirror input** repeats a tap, scroll, text or key on one screen on all the others, as real input.
  - The status bar shows how many devices are in the swarm.
- **Device Matrix.**
  - **View > Device Matrix** shows your app on every screen side by side: the swarm's phones, the App Preview, and virtual devices from a small phone to a tablet, light or dark, left to right or right to left, and with 130% text. Tiles rotate, zoom and open full size.
  - Every screen checks its own layout after each change: clipped text, controls off screen, overlapping controls, touch targets under 44 dips and low contrast. Findings land in the Error List (SWRM001 to SWRM005) with a picture of each screen. Tapping one opens the XAML and outlines the element, and **Fix with Agent** sends Claude the problem, the XAML and the picture.
- **Recorded UI tests.**
  - **Test > Record UI Test** records taps, typing, scrolling and keys on any screen; a long press adds a check. Saving writes an xUnit test into your solution, creating an Avalonia.Headless test project if there is none.
  - The test replays on every screen in Test Explorer, with one row per screen and a picture of the screen where it failed, and runs in CI with `dotnet test`.

## Fixed

- Debug builds of apps that use C# interceptors no longer fail with CS9234.
- After input replayed on Android's web view, the keyboard goes back to the view that had it.

## Install

1. Download `TabStudio-0.10.0.apk` below on your tablet, and on every phone or tablet that will join the swarm.
2. Open it and allow installing apps from your browser or file manager when Android asks.

Requires Android 9 or later on 64-bit or 32-bit ARM, with 3 GB of memory or more (2 GB with TabStudio Link). It installs as an update over 0.9.

## Verified

Every commit runs these checks in CI:

- the unit tests of every library, including the swarm's coordinator, members and cached deployment;
- headless UI tests that type, tap and press keys in the real IDE, with screenshots. They include two phones joined over loopback TLS, where a real tap on one is mirrored to the other, a recorded tap replays by name on the other phone, and both phones' layout findings reach the Error List and the Device Matrix;
- a recorded UI test run with `dotnet test`;
- an APK build with signature verification;
- the app on an Android tablet emulator. There it:
  - runs the sample and answers its question;
  - debugs it to a breakpoint, both on the tablet and through a TabStudio Link host;
  - checks a language server and a debug adapter;
  - serves a new ASP.NET Core app, a Blazor WebAssembly app's boot files and a Blazor Web App with no errors;
  - shows a new Avalonia app;
  - installs a grammar extension from Open VSX and colors its file;
  - installs Python and Node.js and runs a program with each, and debugs Python to a breakpoint;
  - runs a new Windows Forms app, and adds a button to it in the designer;
  - joins a phone to its own swarm over TLS 1.3, runs an app on it, stops at a breakpoint there, and reads the phone's screen;
  - runs desktop mode on a second display.

This release was published by CI only after its own build passed all of them.
