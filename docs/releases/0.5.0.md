TabStudio 0.5 lets a stronger device do the heavy lifting: with TabStudio Link, your phone or PC compiles for your tablet over local Wi-Fi, end-to-end encrypted, with no cloud and no account.

## What's new in 0.5

- **TabStudio Link.**
  - On the phone or PC, open TabStudio and choose **File > TabStudio Link… > Be a host**. It shows a QR code, its address and a one-time code.
  - On the tablet, open the same window, tap **Scan its QR code** (or type the address and code) and pair. Paired hosts are remembered, with **Connect** and **Forget**.
  - While linked, the host checks your code as you type, answers IntelliSense, and builds, including debug builds, Live Unit Testing's builds and the Claude agent's checks. Programs, tests and the debugger still run on the tablet, so a 2 GB tablet can work on large solutions.
  - Only the files that changed travel between the devices. Packages are restored on both.
  - If the host goes away, the tablet keeps working on its own with every unsaved edit, and links again by itself when the host is back. The status bar shows which device is compiling and the latency, for example `Pixel 9 · 12 ms`.
  - **Security:**
    - Everything between the devices is TLS 1.3, or TLS 1.2 on Android 9, and each device checks the other's certificate.
    - Pairing needs the one-time code, which expires after ten minutes and is replaced after a wrong guess.
    - A host compiles with the tablet's packages, analyzers and generators included, so pair only devices you own.

## Fixed

- After linking or reconnecting, the host restores the tablet's packages and adds .NET's source generators before it takes over, so solutions with packages compile there exactly as on the tablet.

## Install

1. Download `TabStudio-0.5.0.apk` below on your tablet, and on the phone that will host.
2. Open it and allow installing apps from your browser or file manager when Android asks. The camera permission is asked the first time you scan a QR code.

Requires Android 9 or later on 64-bit or 32-bit ARM, with 3 GB of memory or more (2 GB with TabStudio Link). It installs as an update over 0.4.

## Verified

Every commit runs these checks in CI:

- the unit tests of every library, including a TLS link over loopback that pairs, builds, debugs and runs Live Unit Testing on the host;
- headless UI tests that type, tap and press keys in the real IDE, with screenshots, including linked errors, completion and rename matching local ones, and a host lost and found again;
- an APK build with signature verification;
- the app on an Android tablet emulator. There it runs the sample and answers its question, debugs it to a breakpoint, pairs with a host over TLS 1.3 and debugs through it, builds on the device after the host leaves, serves a new ASP.NET Core app, shows a new Avalonia app and taps its button, and previews the app's view in the XAML designer.

This release was published by CI only after its own build passed all of them.
