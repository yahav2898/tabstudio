# Code of conduct

TabStudio adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
as its code of conduct.

In short: be welcoming, be patient with people who are new, give feedback on the work and not the person, and
assume good intent. Harassment, insults and personal attacks are not tolerated, in the repository or anywhere
the project is represented.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, contact the maintainers privately through the email
address on the maintainer's GitHub profile. Reports are handled confidentially, and maintainers will act on
them as the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines describe.
